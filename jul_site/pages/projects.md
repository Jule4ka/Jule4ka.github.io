#### Baltic Defence &amp; Geopolitics Monitor
A small data project: I scrape the Baltic public broadcasters' English news
and ask how much of it is actually about defence and security — the share by
country, and the themes underneath. Every matching article is embedded,
coloured by topic and mapped by shared language.
